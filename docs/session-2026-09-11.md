## Price per meter and meters per piece on the worksheet

  mill price converted from its own currency) and **Meters/pc** (the cloth behind
  one garment).
- Meters are divided by the piece count because `fabric_cost_sar` on a row is per
  piece. Quoting the whole line's meters next to a per-piece cost would not

## Accounting skill

- `hagan-erp-accounting` covers the money rules that are enforced in code and
  easy to break:
  - **Two legal entities picked by `delivery_destination`**, not by client
    address: RUH issues as Hagan Industries Company in SAR with 15% VAT, DXB as
    Vitasartoria Ltd payable in DHS with no VAT. `bank-details.ts` owns the IBANs
    and nothing else may copy them.
  - `resolveInvoiceVatRate` returns **`null` for Dubai, not `0`** - a zero would
    render a VAT row claiming zero tax.
  - **5% customs duty is permanent and sits inside `fabric_cost_sar`; 15% import

    as `vat_recoverable_sar` and `fabric_cash_outlay_sar`.

    rows as fully paid. That fallback is intentional.
  - Balance due never goes negative, so overpayments do not surface as credits.
  - `SupplierInvoiceRecord.amount` is a **string parsed from a PDF**, not a
    number. Customs `unknown` is not `pending`.
  - The **accounting role is not admin**: it sees the invoicing and supplier
    billing workflow with every amount redacted. New nested money fields must be
    added to `redactSupplierInvoiceMoney` in the same commit or they leak.
  - There is **no ZATCA e-invoicing** in this repo - verified, zero matches. Do
    not claim compliance or invent QR codes for it.
